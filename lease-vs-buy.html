Lease vs Buy

Both options can be smart — it depends on how you drive, how long you keep a car and what matters most to your budget.

Leasing

Best if you want lower payments and a new car every few years.

Pros

  • Monthly payments 20–30% lower than a loan
  • Always under factory warranty
  • Little or no down payment
  • Easy upgrade to the newest model

Cons

  • Mileage limits and wear-and-tear charges
  • You don’t build equity
  • Ending early is expensive

Buying

Best if you drive a lot and keep your cars for a long time.

Pros

  • The car is yours once the loan is paid off
  • No mileage restrictions
  • Freedom to modify or sell at any time
  • Cheapest option over 6+ years

Cons

  • Higher monthly payments
  • Repairs after the warranty expires
  • Depreciation is your loss

Frequently asked questions

What happens at the end of my lease?

You have three options: return the car and walk away (after an inspection for excess wear and mileage), buy it out for the residual value stated in your contract, or trade it in for a new lease.

Can I end my lease early?

Yes, but early termination usually means paying the remaining depreciation plus a fee. Lease transfer or selling the car to a dealer can be cheaper — contact us and we’ll find the best option.

How many miles can I drive?

Most leases include 10,000, 12,000 or 15,000 miles per year. Extra miles are typically charged at $0.15–$0.30 per mile, so choose a realistic allowance up front.

Do I need full coverage insurance on a leased car?

Yes. Lessors require comprehensive and collision coverage, and we recommend GAP insurance, which covers the difference if the car is totaled or stolen.

Can I lease with bad credit?

It’s possible. A larger down payment, a co-signer or a less expensive model can help you get approved. Our finance team works with lenders for every credit tier.

Is maintenance included?

Routine maintenance isn’t always included, but most new cars come with 2 years of complimentary scheduled service and the factory warranty covers repairs for the entire lease term.
