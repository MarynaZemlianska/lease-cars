An easier way to buy your next Toyota

Skip the haggling: how to compare dealer offers online, lock in a price and arrive only to sign the papers.

Start by choosing the trim and options you actually need — extras such as premium audio or larger wheels can add $50+ to a monthly lease payment. Then request quotes from several certified dealers at once and compare the total cost, not just the monthly figure.

Ask every dealer for the same numbers: capitalized cost, residual value, money factor and fees due at signing. With identical terms, the best offer becomes obvious and you can finish the deal in under an hour.

Lifestyle · 4 min read

A Guide to Road-Tripping With Pets

Crash-tested harnesses, rest-stop planning and how to keep a leased car’s interior in perfect shape.

more

Secure your pet with a crash-tested harness or a carrier fixed to the seat belt, and plan a stop every two to three hours for water and a short walk. Never leave a pet alone in a parked car — the temperature inside rises dangerously fast.

Driving a leased car? Use a waterproof seat cover and cargo liner: they protect against scratches and odors that might count as excess wear when you return the vehicle.
