Why buy certified pre-owned?

Every car in our inventory passes the same strict program before it goes on sale.

  • 160-point inspection

    Engine, brakes, electronics and body are checked and reconditioned by certified technicians.

  • 12-month warranty

    Comprehensive 12-month / 12,000-mile coverage on top of the remaining factory warranty.

  • 7-day return

    Not in love with your car? Return it within 7 days or 300 miles — no questions asked.

  • Free history report

    A full vehicle history report with accidents, owners and service records for every car.
